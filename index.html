How to read this map

Each node is a principal investigator with at least three last-author papers (slider: down to one) in the SRS / coherent-Raman microscopy literature since 2007, from OpenAlex. Conference abstracts and data stubs are hidden unless you tick the box, and the Filters panel hides unrelated fields such as structural vibration or pollution by default. Lines connect PIs who share papers. Nearby nodes collaborate often.

Click a PI to unwind their papers into a chronological string of beads around the node. Click a bead for the paper. Switch to Subjects for papers as beads on a shelf per subject, Geo for the world map, and Lineage for who trained in whose lab.
